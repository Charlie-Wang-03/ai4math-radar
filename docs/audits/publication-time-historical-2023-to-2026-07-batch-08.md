## Critical historical semantics

1. **Google DeepMind:** 2024 AlphaProof / AlphaGeometry 2 *July25* blog and 2025 Nature *Nov12* manuscript are different publication milestones. The original July 2024 blog explicitly labels its own first-public date and points to later methodology paper. Never merge these dates.
2. **Epoch AI:** many linked pages are live dashboards / benchmark detail pages rather than immutable versioned announcement posts. They do not certify an archived day for expansions, correctness repairs, solved-problem updates, removals or o3 benchmark score releases. Do not add chronology on guessed dates. Epoch's **Jan27 2026** dated original pilot post is separately verifiable.
3. **arXiv:** submission-click date, article version, daily email announcement, external news and conference presentation have different timestamps. Exact public `announced` moment requires per-item evidence; no blanket global schedule backfill.
4. **Nature:** Nov12 publication of AlphaProof paper is explicitly a separate public academic artifact from the 2024 Google blog. Distinguish paper publication from initial public experiment result.
5. **NeurIPS 2023 Lean Copilot:** public workshop schedule confirms event day but not a specifically dated release milestone for Lean Copilot; avoid treating the workshop schedule as proof of source repository publication time.
6. **First Proof:** current first-batch site is an undated historical archive; Feb5 needs original challenge launch metadata before upgrading date or precision.

- Retrieve historic snapshot releases of Epoch dashboard data, GitHub tag/release archives and author announcements; match exact claim and version before assigning event dates.
- Retrieve original arXiv paper announcement/first posting fields (not submit-click), and per-version scholarly repository records.

- If first-public evidence has a different UTC day, preserve immutable event ID and store `chronology.earliestVerifiedPublic` separately from the primary reported milestone.
